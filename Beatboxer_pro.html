BeatBorder

Your screen edges, dancing to every beat. A Windows app that wraps your display in a glowing gradient synced to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube — and even the gunshots in your games.

Get it running

Windows 10 or 11 with Python 3.10+ (tick “Add Python to PATH” when installing).

pip install PySide6 numpy PyAudioWPatch pythonw beatborder_pro.py

Play a song and the borders light up. Right-click the tray icon for quick modes, or double-click it to open settings. For games, use borderless windowed mode, since exclusive fullscreen hides overlays. Flashing effects can be intense, so lower Flash intensity if it bothers your eyes.
